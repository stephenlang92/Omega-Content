### Snippet 3: Lộ trình Hành động 4 Tháng (The 120-Day Milestone Playbook) `[ENRICH]`
*(Chèn vào mục "How Black-Owned Businesses Can Maximize Shopify’s 120-Day Trial")*

> **The 120-Day Milestone Playbook:**
> - **Month 1 (Days 1–30): Foundation & Store Architecture:**  
>   Focus on branding, setting up your domain, configuring shipping zones, and uploading your initial 5–10 core products with high-resolution imagery. Complete the Operation HOPE onboarding session.
> - **Month 2 (Days 31–60): Soft Launch & Customer Feedback:**  
>   Open your store to family, friends, and your immediate network. Process your first 10 orders to stress-test your packaging, shipping fulfillment, and payment gateway.
> - **Month 3 (Days 61–90): Organic Traffic & Community Building:**  
>   Leverage short-form video on TikTok and Instagram Reels showing your product creation story. Build an email welcome flow to capture store visitors.
> - **Month 4 (Days 91–120): Conversion Optimization & Scaling:**  
>   Analyze your conversion funnel using Shopify Analytics. Implement trust badges and automated tracking notifications. Calculate your profit margins to ensure your sales cover the standard \$39/month (or \$29/month annual) Basic plan once day 121 arrives.

> **A:** When your 120 days conclude, your store will not be automatically charged unless you actively choose a subscription plan. You have three flexible options:  
> 1. **Upgrade to a paid plan:** Continue scaling your store with Shopify's Basic Plan (\$39/month on monthly billing, or \$29/month if billed annually).
