When creating a PayPal account, you might wonder whether you need a Personal or a Business account—and what happened to the 'third type' you've heard about. In 2026, PayPal officially offers **two primary account categories**: Personal and Business. However, many guides still reference a third tier—historically known as PayPal Premier (which was merged into Personal/Business) or PayPal Payments Pro (now modernized into PayPal Complete Payments). In this comprehensive guide, we'll break down the key differences between these options so you can choose the exact account that matches your personal or e-commerce needs.

PayPal is increasing its number of users every year; it is a convenient way for users to pay and receive money online, and for businesses, it works as a trusted global payment gateway to accept payments from customers around the world. Knowing which type of PayPal account you should choose before opening an account is crucial to take advantage of its benefits.

## **3 Types of PayPal Accounts**

When you sign up on PayPal today, there are **2 primary active account types** to choose from: **Personal account** and **Business account**. Additionally, for high-volume merchants and scaling enterprises, there is an advanced commercial tier historically known as **PayPal Payments Pro** (now integrated under **PayPal Complete Payments / Commerce Platform**).

![](https://strapi.omegatheme.com/uploads/paypal_type_ok1_60c1a704ac.png)

### **1. Personal account**

If your main purpose is just **paying money online** or sending and requesting money from your friends and families, a Paypal personal account is appropriate for you. In addition, a Personal account also can receive money if you sell goods and services online with a low fee.

However, when you use a Personal account to sell, your name will be shown on each transaction which affects your privacy. In addition, selling by using a Personal account will limit the form of payments. A PayPal personal account will be an ideal option for people who sell their goods and services occasionally.

**Key Pros & Limitations of a Personal Account:**
- **Pros:** Free to open ($0 monthly fee), instant peer-to-peer (P2P) transfers with friends and family, eligible for PayPal Buyer Protection and reward cashback programs.
- **Limitations:** Cannot integrate as a standalone checkout gateway on eCommerce platforms like Shopify, displays your legal personal name on buyer bank statements, and offers no multi-user employee access.

[How to set up a Paypal personal account](https://www.paypal.com/us/digital-wallet/how-paypal-works)

### **2. Business account**

If your purpose is to sell products & services online, a Paypal business account is designed for you to receive payment from your customers. A Business account includes all the benefits of a Personal account and more:

- If your customers do not have a Paypal account, it also allows you to accept payments by credit card, debit card, and local payment methods (including Pay Later options)
- You can operate the account under your registered company or brand name, which helps protect your personal privacy
- It allows up to 200 employees to access your business account with customizable role-based permissions to manage finance, refunds, and fulfillment
- It allows you to send commercial invoices, print shipping labels, track payments, and integrate directly with eCommerce platforms like Shopify, WooCommerce, and Wix

[How to set up a Paypal business account](https://www.paypal.com/us/business/open-business-account)

### **3. Paypal Payment Pro (Paypal Pro)**

Basically, Paypal Pro was designed as an enterprise underwriting upgrade to a standard Business account, giving merchants all the benefits of a Business account plus the ability to host a fully customized, white-label credit card checkout directly on their website and accept Virtual Terminal payments through phone, fax, and mail.

**2026 Update — Transition to PayPal Complete Payments:** In recent years, PayPal has evolved legacy *PayPal Payments Pro* into **PayPal Complete Payments** (and the *PayPal Commerce Platform*). Rather than a standalone signup tier, it now functions as an advanced merchant solution for established businesses that require custom on-site checkout flows, advanced fraud protection, international multi-currency settlement, and dedicated API controls to optimize cash flow.

[How to get started with Paypal Pro](https://www.paypal.com/ca/smarthelp/article/how-do-i-get-started-with-paypal-payments-pro-faq1075)

## **Tips for using Paypal accounts**

- Verify your Paypal account, verifying by Visa card or linking your confirmed bank account will help your Paypal account work stably and lift initial sending and withdrawal limits.
- **Avoid the 21-Day Payment Hold & Disputes with Automated Tracking:** For new Business accounts or sudden spikes in sales, PayPal often places funds on a temporary 21-day hold to ensure order fulfillment. To get your funds released faster and win "Item Not Received" disputes automatically, always upload valid carrier tracking numbers to PayPal as soon as an order ships (Shopify merchants can automate this in real time using order tracking sync apps like **Omega Synctrack**).
- After receiving money from other accounts, do not send or withdraw money immediately to avoid suspicion from Paypal. You should wait for 30 minutes minimum to send money and withdraw after at least 1 day. If you have a large amount of money, it is strongly recommended to transfer it back to your bank account.
- Do not abuse the "Friends and Family" fee-free transfer option for commercial sales; doing so violates PayPal's Acceptable Use Policy, revokes Seller Protection, and can trigger permanent account limitations or tax compliance audits.
- Keep your account with clear transaction history: Following are some of the cases Paypal limit your account, check and avoid this:

  - Change IP address when you log in continuously
  - Do log in & log out activity many times
  - Happen a sudden change in your account (ex: receive or withdraw a big amount of money in a short time)
  - Send or receive money from a suspicious account
  - Add or remove your bank card frequently, change or remove email, password, or security questions many times

Even though a person can own 2 accounts (1 personal & 1 business), choosing an appropriate account type can benefit your personal use or your business. In addition, if you are using a personal account, you can upgrade to a business account, and from a business account to an advanced Complete Payments/Pro setup, though downgrading a Business account back to Personal typically requires contacting PayPal Support or opening a separate personal email account.

## **Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)**

**Q: Can I have both a Personal and a Business PayPal account?**  
**A:** Yes. PayPal officially allows you to hold one Personal account and one Business account simultaneously, provided each account is linked to a separate email address and bank account.

**Q: Is it free to upgrade from a Personal to a Business account?**  
**A:** Yes, upgrading from Personal to Business is completely free. You only pay standard transaction fees when receiving commercial payments.

**Q: Can I use a PayPal Personal account for my Shopify store?**  
**A:** No. Shopify requires a PayPal Business account to capture automated payments. Using a Personal account for business transactions violates PayPal's User Agreement and risks immediate account limitation or payment holds.

**Q: Why does PayPal hold funds on new Business accounts?**  
**A:** For new or unverified sellers, PayPal often applies a 21-day temporary hold to protect buyers. You can expedite fund release within 24 hours of delivery confirmation by promptly uploading valid carrier tracking numbers for every fulfilled order.
