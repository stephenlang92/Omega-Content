## **Alternative Shopify Plans**

**![](https://strapi.omegatheme.com/uploads/Alternative_Shopify_Plans_a965b00532.png)**

### **Shopify Starter Plan**

[Shopify Starter](https://shopify.pxf.io/7amxNr), at **$5 per month** (annual plan), lets you sell instantly on social media and messaging apps without needing a full website. Perfect for entrepreneurs testing products or businesses with existing sites, it includes Shopify’s order management, sales analytics, and app market access.

![](https://strapi.omegatheme.com/uploads/Shopify_Starter_Plan_25a08e92e5.png)

Key Features:

- **Social Selling:** Share products on Instagram, WhatsApp, and more, with Shopify’s secure checkout.
- **Link in Bio Monetization:** Create a Linkpop page to direct followers to products via your bio link.
- **Order Management:** Handle orders easily from your admin.
- **Analytics:** Track sales and customer insights.
- **Support:** Access Shopify’s customer support to help you grow.

**Limitations:** This plan has only product pages, a contact page, and checkout. Upgrade to Basic for more customization, blogs, and discounts.

### **Shopify Retail Plan**

[Shopify Retail plan](https://shopify.pxf.io/LKPrNj), ideal for businesses focused on in-person sales, costs **$89/month** (after a $1 first month). It offers a simple web store with advanced tools for staff, inventory, and loyalty.

![](https://strapi.omegatheme.com/uploads/Shopify_Retail_Plan_c5adeb207c.png)

Key Features:

- Secure payments & fast payouts
- Unlimited registers & unified inventory
- Cash tracking & split payments
- Gift cards (physical and digital)
- Free Shopify POS app
- Refunds & returns
- 24/7 support

### **Shopify Plus**

Designed for large, fast-growing businesses like Mattel and Sony, [Shopify Plus](https://shopify.pxf.io/yqRL0W) offers scalable, customizable solutions for enterprise-level sales. Starting at $2,300/month, pricing varies based on needs, requiring a custom quote from Shopify. This plan targets businesses with over $750,000 in annual revenue.

![](https://strapi.omegatheme.com/uploads/Shopify_Plus_Pricing_fe436448be.png)

Key Features:

- Competitive rates for high-volume sales
- Custom reports & analytics
- 200 inventory locations
- 24/7 priority support
- Localized selling in 50 markets
- Unlimited staff accounts
- Fully customizable checkout with 40x capacity
- 200 POS Pro locations
- Wholesale/B2B sales
