### **Additional & Hidden Costs You Must Budget For**

Beyond monthly plans, budget for these 5 common operational expenses:

- **Custom Domain ($14–$25/year):** A branded `.com` domain is essential for customer trust.
- **Credit Card Processing (2.4%–2.9% + 30¢):** Standard transaction fees per online order on Shopify Payments.
- **Third-Party Gateway Fee (0.6%–2%):** Additional fee if using external processors instead of Shopify Payments.
- **Premium Themes ($180–$400 one-time):** Optional paid templates for advanced design and features.
- **Essential Apps ($30–$150/month):** Key add-ons for reviews, email marketing, and automated tracking sync.

### **When Should You Upgrade Your Plan? (Breakeven Analysis)**

Many merchants wonder when it makes financial sense to upgrade from **Basic ($39/month)** to the **Shopify Grow plan ($105/month)**, or from **Shopify ($105/month)** to **Advanced ($399/month)** based purely on credit card processing savings:

- **Upgrading from Basic ($39) to Shopify ($105):** The monthly subscription cost increases by **$66/month** ($50/month on annual billing), while your online credit card rate drops by **0.3%** (from 2.9% down to 2.6%).  
  $$\text{Breakeven Monthly Revenue} = \frac{\$66}{0.003} = \$22,000\text{/month (Monthly Billing)}$$  
  *(Or **$16,667/month** on Annual Billing where the price difference is $50/month. If you use a third-party gateway where the fee drops by 1.0% from 2.0% to 1.0%, your breakeven point is just **$6,600/month**!)*
- **Upgrading from Shopify ($105) to Advanced ($399):** The monthly subscription cost increases by **$294/month** ($220/month on annual billing), while your online card rate drops by **0.2%** (from 2.6% down to 2.4%). That means transaction fee savings alone offset the upgrade once your store processes **$110,000 to $147,000/month** in online sales—though many brands upgrade earlier for custom reporting and 15 staff accounts.

## **Frequently Asked Questions About Shopify Pricing**

**Q1: Does Shopify take a cut or percentage of my sales?**  
**A:** If you use Shopify Payments, Shopify does not take an additional platform cut beyond the standard credit card processing fee (2.9% + 30¢ on Basic, 2.6% + 30¢ on Shopify, and 2.4% + 30¢ on Advanced). However, if you use an external third-party payment gateway, Shopify charges an additional transaction fee of 2.0% (Basic), 1.0% (Shopify), or 0.6% (Advanced).

**Q2: When should I upgrade from the Basic plan to the Shopify plan?**  
**A:** The primary financial benefit of upgrading is the 0.3% drop in credit card processing fees (from 2.9% down to 2.6%) and the 1.0% drop in third-party transaction fees. Doing the math, once your monthly revenue exceeds **$16,500 to $22,000** (depending on whether you pay annually or monthly), the price difference between Basic and Shopify is completely offset by transaction fee savings.

**Q3: Can I change or cancel my Shopify plan at any time?**  
**A:** Yes. If you are on a monthly billing plan, you can upgrade, downgrade, pause, or cancel your store at any time without a long-term commitment. If you opted for an annual prepaid plan to capture the 25% discount, plan downgrades take effect at the end of your 12-month billing cycle.

**Q4: Is there a completely free Shopify plan in 2026?**  
**A:** Shopify does not offer a permanent free plan for live stores, but new merchants can start with a **3-day free trial** without a credit card, followed by the **$1/month for your first 3 months** promotional offer—giving you over 90 days to build and test your store for just $3 total.
