### Snippet 3: Khối Bóc tách Chi phí Ẩn & Phụ phí `[FILL-GAP (+189 từ)]`
*(Chèn thẻ H3: "Additional & Hidden Costs You Must Budget For")*

> **The True Cost of Running a Shopify Store Beyond Subscription Fees:**
> 1. **Custom Domain Name (\$14 – \$25/year):** Your store comes with a free `yourname.myshopify.com` URL, but establishing customer trust requires a custom `.com` domain purchased through Shopify or third-party registrars like Namecheap.
> 2. **Online Payment Processing Fees (2.4% to 2.9% + 30¢):** Every time a customer pays by credit card via Shopify Payments, a standard processing fee is deducted from your payout. For example, on a \$100 sale with the Basic plan, you pay \$3.20 in processing.
> 3. **Third-Party Payment Gateway Penalties (0.6% to 2.0%):** If you choose not to use Shopify Payments and instead process via standalone PayPal, Stripe, or Authorize.net, Shopify charges an additional 2% fee on Basic (1% on Shopify, 0.6% on Advanced).
> 4. **Premium Store Theme (\$180 – \$400 one-time):** While Shopify offers solid free themes (like Dawn), scaling brands often invest in premium Shopify Theme Store templates for advanced merchandising and faster page load speeds.
> 5. **Essential Third-Party Apps (\$30 – \$150/month):** Most merchants require 3–5 dedicated apps for email marketing (Klaviyo), reviews (Judge.me), and automated tracking sync (Synctrack to avoid PayPal disputes).

### Snippet 4: Khối FAQ chuẩn PAA `[FILL-GAP (+170 từ)]`
*(Chèn thẻ H2: "Frequently Asked Questions About Shopify Pricing")*

> **Q1: Does Shopify take a cut or percentage of my sales?**  
> **A:** If you use Shopify Payments, Shopify does not take an additional cut beyond the standard credit card processing fee (2.9% + 30¢ on Basic). However, if you use a third-party payment gateway, Shopify charges an additional transaction fee of 2.0% (Basic), 1.0% (Shopify), or 0.6% (Advanced).
>
> **Q2: When should I upgrade from the Basic plan to the Shopify plan?**  
> **A:** The primary financial benefit of upgrading is the 0.3% drop in credit card fees (from 2.9% down to 2.6%). Doing the math, once your monthly sales exceed **\$16,500**, the \$66 price difference between Basic (\$39) and Shopify (\$105) is completely offset by transaction savings.
>
> **Q3: Can I change or cancel my Shopify plan at any time?**  
> **A:** Yes. If you are on a monthly billing plan, you can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your store at any time without long-term commitment. If you opted for an annual prepaid plan, downgrades apply at the end of the 12-month billing cycle.
