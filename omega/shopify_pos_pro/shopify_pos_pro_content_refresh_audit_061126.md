### Snippet 4: Bổ sung câu hỏi thường gặp bám sát Google US People Also Ask `[FILL-GAP]`
> Bổ sung thêm 3 câu hỏi sau vào khối FAQ hiện có:
>
> > **Q4: Can I use Shopify POS Pro offline if my retail store loses internet connectivity?**  
> > **A:** Yes. Shopify POS Pro features an **Offline Mode** that allows cashiers to continue processing transactions and accepting cash payments even during an internet outage. Card payments can also be queued depending on your card reader configuration. All offline sales data automatically syncs with your centralized Shopify admin the moment connectivity is restored.
> >
> > **Q5: Can I mix and match POS Lite and POS Pro across different locations?**  
> > **A:** Absolutely. Shopify allows location-specific subscriptions. For example, if you operate a permanent flagship store and occasionally run a temporary weekend farmers' market or pop-up stall, you can activate POS Pro ($89/mo) for your flagship location while using free POS Lite for your mobile pop-up events.
> >
> > **Q6: Are there long-term contract commitments for Shopify POS Pro?**  
> > **A:** No. Shopify POS Pro is billed on a flexible 30-day rolling cycle. You can activate or downgrade POS Pro for any specific retail location at any time directly inside your Shopify admin dashboard without paying cancellation penalties.
