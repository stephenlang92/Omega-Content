In traditional eCommerce, transparent pricing is considered a standard best practice. However, for B2B wholesalers, luxury brands, and custom-order manufacturers, displaying public price tags can destroy profit margins and trigger price wars with competitors.

From adhering to strict Minimum Advertised Price (MAP) manufacturer agreements to creating exclusive 'members-only' wholesale pricing tiers, controlling price visibility is a vital commercial strategy.

In this comprehensive guide, we explore the strategic benefits of **hiding product prices**, when you should (and shouldn't) hide them, and how to implement gated pricing seamlessly on Shopify.

## **What is Hide Price?**

As anyone knows in eCommerce, every product has an individual page and **the price will display on both sides of the product page and collection page**. It also appears in the Search Tab, Recently Viewed Product (etc) if available. 

The name speaks for itself, Hide Price is the action of making a product’s price disappear and preventing customers from viewing this information when they are shopping in your online store. Instead of getting the product price in your store, customers need to contact the seller for a quotation via email, text, or phone call. 

![](https://strapi.omegatheme.com/uploads/quote_blog_2_36ce1e97ab.jpg)

### **What is MAP Pricing and Why Does It Force Retailers to Hide Prices?**

**MAP (Minimum Advertised Price)** is a policy enforced by brand manufacturers prohibiting authorized retailers from advertising products below a specified price point.
- Violating MAP can result in authorized sellers losing their distribution rights.
- **The Legal Workaround:** MAP regulations typically govern *advertised* public prices on product listing pages. Retailers remain legally permitted to sell items at lower rates behind closed doors. By utilizing "Click to View Price in Cart" or hiding prices behind a mandatory customer login, eCommerce stores maintain competitive wholesale discounts while remaining 100% MAP compliant.

### **When Should You NOT Hide Prices? (Strategic Decision Matrix)**

Hiding prices is a powerful B2B lever, but using it inappropriately on impulse retail goods will cause cart abandonment. Use this framework:

| Business Model / Product Type | Recommended Strategy | Primary Business Reason |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **Everyday B2C Impulse Goods** (Apparel, everyday gadgets) | **Show Price Publicly** | Shoppers expect instant checkout; hiding prices creates unnecessary friction and drops conversion rates. |
| **B2B Wholesale / Bulk Orders** (Tiered volume discounts) | **Hide Price (Login to View)** | Protects trade pricing from retail consumers and shields wholesale margins from competitors. |
| **Custom / Made-to-Order Items** (Industrial equipment, custom jewelry) | **Hide Price & Add "Request a Quote"** | Costs fluctuate based on materials, volume, and specifications; fixed prices are inaccurate. |
| **MAP-Restricted Brand Merchandise** | **Hide Price Until Login / Cart** | Maintains manufacturer authorization while allowing private discounting for VIP members. |

### **How to Implement Hide Price on Shopify**

#### **Method 1: Manual Liquid & CSS Editing (Developer Path)**
If you are comfortable editing your Shopify theme code:
- To hide prices for unauthenticated visitors, locate your `price.liquid` snippet and wrap the pricing block inside a customer authentication condition:
  ```liquid
  {% if customer %}
    <span class="price">{{ product.price | money }}</span>
  {% else %}
    <a href="/account/login" class="login-for-price">Log in to view wholesale price</a>
  {% endif %}
  ```
- *Limitation:* Manual code requires ongoing maintenance, breaks whenever you update your Shopify theme, and does not automatically generate quote request workflows.

## **Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)**

### **Does hiding product prices hurt my Google SEO rankings?**
No, hiding prices does not directly penalize your Google organic ranking. However, if your product pages have structured data (Schema markup) declaring an empty or `0.00` price while hiding it on the page, Google Merchant Center may flag a mismatch. When hiding prices on-page, you should configure your schema to omit the `offers.price` property or classify the listing as an inquiry/quote page rather than a direct checkout product.

### **How do I show prices only to approved B2B wholesale buyers on Shopify?**
The most effective method is using customer tags (e.g., `wholesale`). With an app like [QuoteSnap](https://apps.shopify.com/request-for-quote-by-omega), you can set a rule that automatically shows prices and the Add to Cart button only when a user logs in with an authorized wholesale tag, while displaying a "Log in to View Pricing" or "Request a Quote" button to everyone else.

### **Can I hide prices on specific product collections while leaving others public?**
Yes. You do not need to hide prices across your entire store. You can selectively hide prices on high-ticket machinery, custom-built products, or exclusive collections while maintaining standard self-checkout on your everyday retail inventory.
