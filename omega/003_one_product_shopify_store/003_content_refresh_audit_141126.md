### Snippet 7: Khối FAQ bổ sung cuối bài `[FILL-GAP (+280 từ)]`
> **## FAQs About One-Product Shopify Stores**
>
> **Q: How much does it cost to start a one-product Shopify store in 2026?**  
> **A:** You can launch with a minimal budget: a Shopify Basic plan ($39/month, or $1/month during promotional trial periods), a custom domain (~$15/year), and a free Shopify Online Store 2.0 theme like Dawn or Sense ($0). Your primary variable cost will be product samples, photography, and initial ad testing.
>
> **Q: Is a one-product store better than a general store on Shopify?**  
> **A:** A one-product store is generally better if you want to build a recognizable brand, achieve higher conversion rates through focused messaging, and simplify inventory management. General stores work best only for rapid product testing before transitioning winning items into dedicated branded stores.
>
> **Q: Can I add more products later if my store grows?**  
> **A:** Yes. Many successful Shopify brands, such as BlendJet and TUSHY, started with a single hero product and later expanded into complementary accessories, refills, and bundles to increase customer lifetime value (LTV).
