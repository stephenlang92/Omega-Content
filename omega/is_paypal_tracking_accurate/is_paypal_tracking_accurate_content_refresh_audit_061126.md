### Snippet 2: Giải thích cơ chế kỹ thuật và nguyên nhân sai lệch `[ENRICH]`
*(Chèn thẻ H3: "Is PayPal Tracking Accurate? How PayPal Pulls Data & Why Delays Happen")*

> **How PayPal Tracking Works Behind the Scenes:**
> When you enter a tracking number into PayPal, the platform connects to the courier's tracking database via automated Webhooks and APIs. Once the carrier registers an event (such as *In Transit, Out for Delivery,* or *Delivered*), the status updates on the buyer's transaction details.
>
> **Why Tracking Inaccuracies and Discrepancies Occur:**
> 1. **Carrier API Sync Lag:** While major couriers like USPS update their local servers instantly upon barcode scan, third-party API webhooks sent to PayPal can lag by several hours during peak shopping seasons.
> 2. **Incorrect Carrier Name Selection:** A frequent human error occurs when sellers input a tracking number but select the wrong courier name in the drop-down menu (e.g., selecting USPS instead of UPS, or using a generic "Other"). In this scenario, PayPal's automated system cannot query the correct carrier API, leaving the shipment marked as "In Transit" indefinitely.
> 3. **Carrier Tracking Recycling:** In rare cases, logistics providers recycle tracking numbers from months prior. If an old tracking number is entered, PayPal may temporarily show historical delivery scans until the carrier purges the previous manifest.
> 4. **Fake or Mismatched Zip Code Scams:** Scammers sometimes purchase invalid tracking numbers that show delivery within the same city but not to the buyer's exact address. To combat this, PayPal's updated 2026 algorithms rigorously cross-reference recipient postal codes against shipping manifests.

### Snippet 4: Khối FAQ chuẩn PAA `[FILL-GAP]`
*(Chèn thẻ H2: "Frequently Asked Questions About PayPal Tracking")*

> **Q1: Does PayPal verify tracking numbers with the shipping carrier?**  
> **A:** Yes. PayPal actively verifies tracking numbers with carrier APIs. When a tracking number is submitted, PayPal pings the carrier system to confirm that the package was dispatched and that the destination postal code matches the buyer’s registered shipping address.
>
> **Q2: Why does PayPal tracking say "Delivered" but the buyer claims they never received it?**  
> **A:** This usually happens due to carrier misdelivery, porch piracy, or a local postal delay where the carrier scanned the parcel early. Under PayPal Seller Protection, as long as your tracking shows an official "Delivered" scan to the buyer's address (with signature confirmation for orders over $750), PayPal will side with the seller in an INR dispute.
>
> **Q3: How long does it take for PayPal to release funds after tracking shows delivered?**  
> **A:** If your funds are placed on a standard 21-day hold, PayPal typically releases the money within **24 hours** after the carrier tracking confirms successful delivery to the customer.
>
> **Q4: Can a buyer win a dispute if a fake tracking number was used?**  
> **A:** Yes. If a seller provides a fictitious tracking number or a tracking number that delivers to a different postal code, PayPal's dispute resolution team will review the carrier manifest and grant a full refund to the buyer.
