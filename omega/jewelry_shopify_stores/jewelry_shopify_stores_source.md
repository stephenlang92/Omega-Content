## How to Sell Jewelry on Shopify?

### 1. Create Your Shopify Account

Visit the [OmegaTheme partner link](https://shopify.pxf.io/Zday50?utm_source=omegaweb&utm_medium=click_link_aff) to sign up and click “Start Free Trial” to access the $1 for 3 months promotion.

Enter your email address, create a password, and set a store name.

Provide information about your business, such as the type of products you plan to sell and your expected sales volume.

![](https://strapi.omegatheme.com/uploads/Omega_Partner_link_with_Shopify_d939025f1d.png)

### 2. Choose and Customize a Theme

Go to the Theme Store of Shopify and check for [themes that are made for the jewelry stores](https://www.omegatheme.com/blogs/top-shopify-jewelry-themes) or the premium ones. Examples of such themes are “Prestige” and “Minimal”.

Once you found your favorite theme then click to “Add Theme” to your store.

Use the drag-and-drop editor of Shopify to:

- Upload your logo and specify your brand colors,
- Organize the layout to feature jewelry images and categories, add banners or sliders for promotional or bestsellers.

>>> Read more: [13 Best Free Shopify Themes Guaranteed to Boost Your Sales](https://www.omegatheme.com/blogs/best-free-shopify-themes-to-boost-your-sales)

![](https://strapi.omegatheme.com/uploads/Shopify_theme_store_da96f18ba7.png)

### 3. Add Jewelry Products

On the Shopify dashboard, click “Add Product.”

Include multiple images for each piece of jewelry, such as close-ups, side angles, and worn views. Use professional lighting to capture the sparkle and details.

Add a detailed yet concise description covering:

- Material (e.g., gold, silver, diamond).
- Dimensions and weight.
- Certifications (e.g., GIA-certified diamonds).

Enter competitive pricing and consider offering variants for size, color, or design.

Use collections like “Necklaces,” “Rings,” or “Earrings” to make navigation easier for customers.

![](https://strapi.omegatheme.com/uploads/Jewelry_Products_1291fc6e2d.png)

### **4. Set Up Payment Methods**

In your Shopify admin panel, choose the payment options you want to enable.

This option supports credit cards and reduces transaction fees.

Link popular payment gateways like PayPal, Stripe, or Apple Pay to cater to a wider audience.

![](https://strapi.omegatheme.com/uploads/Set_up_Shopify_payments_fd2f706628.png)

### **5. Configure Shipping Options**

Go to “Settings” > “Shipping and Delivery.” Set flat rates, free shipping thresholds, or calculated rates based on weight or location.

Specify the regions or countries where you’ll ship products.

Include express shipping or local delivery for customers who need faster service.

### **6. Test and Launch Your Store**

Check the desktop and mobile versions of your store to ensure everything looks good.

Place a test order to confirm that payments, shipping calculations, and email notifications are working.

Once everything is ready, remove any placeholder content and click “Publish” to make your store live.
