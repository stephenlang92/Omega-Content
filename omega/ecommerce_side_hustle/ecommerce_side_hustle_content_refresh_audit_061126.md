### Snippet 3: Khối bổ sung Chiến lược Quản lý Thời gian (The 9-to-5 Rule) `[ENRICH (+121 từ)]`
*(Chèn vào mục "How to Identify the Ideal E-commerce Side Hustle?")*

> **The 9-to-5 Survival Playbook: How to Protect Your Job & Scale Your Side Gig:**
> 1. **Establish Strict Boundaries:** Never work on your side hustle during official company hours or on company-issued laptops. Review your employment contract regarding intellectual property and non-compete clauses to ensure zero conflicts of interest.
> 2. **Batching Your Workflow:** Instead of checking orders every 30 minutes, designate two 45-minute blocks each day (e.g., 7:30 AM before work and 7:00 PM after dinner) to answer customer inquiries and fulfill orders.
> 3. **Automate Ruthlessly with Shopify Apps:** As order volume grows, manual tasks will drain your energy. Automate order confirmation emails, inventory tracking, and payment synchronization (using tools like Synctrack) so your store functions 24/7 without requiring your active presence.

### Snippet 4: Khối FAQ bổ sung chuẩn PAA `[FILL-GAP (+120 từ)]`
*(Chèn vào thẻ H2: "FAQ about E-commerce Side Hustles")*

> **Q4: How much money do I realistically need to start an e-commerce side hustle?**  
> **A:** You can launch a low-overhead model like Digital Downloads or Print-on-Demand with as little as **\$50 to \$150** (primarily covering a basic Shopify subscription, domain registration, and initial mockups). Models requiring physical inventory or heavy ad spend (like Amazon FBA or wholesale) typically require \$1,500 to \$3,000 in upfront capital.
>
> **Q5: How many hours per week should I spend on an online side business?**  
> **A:** Most successful part-time merchants dedicate **8 to 15 hours per week**. Consistency is far more impactful than marathon sessions—spending 1.5 to 2 hours every evening and 3 hours on Saturday morning is sufficient to launch and maintain a profitable store.
