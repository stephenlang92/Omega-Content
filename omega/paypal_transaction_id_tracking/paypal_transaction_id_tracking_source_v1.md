## **What is a PayPal tracking number?**

A PayPal tracking number is a chain of digits that can include numbers and letters or only numbers from a carrier. However, this is different from transaction ID which normally first appears on your invoice or order information.

All PayPal transaction IDs have unique 17-characters used to identify each transaction. You can use the PayPal transaction ID to track the status of a transaction or to search for past transactions. You can get transaction details by following  these steps:  
1- Access your PayPal account.  
2- Click the Activities menu option.  
3- Select Transaction ID in the search drop-down menu.  
4- Enter the transaction ID and press enter.

To clarify the fundamental distinction between these two codes, review the comparison table below:

| Comparison Criteria | PayPal Transaction ID | Carrier Tracking Number |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **Code Format** | 17-character alphanumeric string (e.g., `9AB12345CD67890EF`) | Numeric or alphanumeric postal code (e.g., USPS 22-digit `9400...`, UPS 18-character `1Z...`) |
| **Issuing Entity** | Generated by PayPal's payment gateway immediately upon checkout completion | Issued by the shipping courier (USPS, FedEx, DHL, Royal Mail, 4PX, etc.) when the shipping label is created |
| **Primary Purpose** | Identifies the payment invoice, searches financial transaction history, and opens Resolution Center disputes | Tracks the physical geographical transit and delivery progress of a shipped parcel |
| **Where to Look It Up?** | Searchable only inside the PayPal website/app or when contacting PayPal Customer Support | Searchable on carrier tracking portals, the merchant's order tracking page, or multi-carrier platforms like 17TRACK |
| **How Are They Linked?** | The seller must **attach the Carrier Tracking Number to the PayPal Transaction ID** inside PayPal so both parties are protected | Linked directly to the PayPal transaction record once uploaded by the merchant |

## **Where customers can find a tracking number**

For any shipping product, every seller needs to [add tracking info to Paypal](https://www.paypal.com/us/cshelp/article/how-do-i-track-a-shipment--help1070) including a tracking number so that their customers can know where their item is. Normally tracking numbers can be found on customers’ Paypal page, email from Paypal, or email from third-party platforms. In some special cases, online stores like Walmart and Amazon don’t provide tracking numbers for specific packages, so you won’t see them in Paypal Shipping Center.

Besides Paypal tracking, customers can also have tracking numbers if they buy products on e-commerce platforms such as Shopify tracking number, WooCommerce Shipment Tracking, and so on.

Specifically, buyers can check three primary locations to retrieve their shipment tracking code:
1. **PayPal Payment & Shipment Confirmation Emails:** As soon as the merchant uploads fulfillment details to PayPal, PayPal automatically sends an email titled *"Your order has shipped"* containing the carrier name and clickable tracking link.
2. **The Activity Menu on PayPal Web or Mobile App:** Log into your PayPal account, navigate to **Activity**, click on the specific purchase using your 17-character Transaction ID, and scroll down to the **Shipping Details / Tracking** section.
3. **Merchant Store Order Updates:** Check the order confirmation email or SMS sent directly by the online store (e.g., via Shopify order status links), which often updates even before PayPal receives the batch sync.

The customer can see the progress of their shipment on their Paypal Activity page. For each order, the details of the shipping address, shipping service, and the date shipped are all shown. If they cannot see, they either can contact sellers or Paypal to get details.

If the customers aren’t a member of Paypal, from a tracking number received by email or on the order information, they can simply go to the website of the shipping company (for example USPS, 4PX, Royal Mail, etc) and enter the tracking number to check.

For cross-border shipments originating from international warehouses (such as packages shipped via YunExpress, CJ Packet, 4PX, or DHL eCommerce that are later handed off to local postal services like USPS or Royal Mail), carrier portals may occasionally show a delay during customs clearance or local handoff. ## **Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)**

**Q: Can I enter a PayPal Transaction ID into USPS Tracking?**  
**A:** No. USPS and other freight couriers cannot recognize PayPal internal transaction identifiers. You must open your PayPal account, click into the transaction details, locate the actual USPS tracking number provided by the seller, and paste that code into the USPS tracking portal.

**Q: Why is there no tracking number visible in my PayPal transaction details?**  
**A:** If you don't see a tracking number, it typically means the seller has not dispatched the order yet, they forgot to enter the tracking details into PayPal, or the item purchased was a digital service/good that requires no physical shipping. You can message the seller directly via the transaction page to request tracking.

**Q: How long does PayPal hold funds if a seller provides valid tracking?**  
**A:** Under standard PayPal policies, funds are held for 21 days. However, when a valid tracking number is submitted and the carrier confirms delivery, PayPal automatically releases the held balance to the merchant's available balance within **24 hours of delivery confirmation**.

**Q: What should I do if a seller inputs an invalid or fake tracking number?**  
**A:** If the tracking number provided remains inactive after several days or points to a completely different destination address, go to PayPal's **Resolution Center** within 180 days of payment and open an "Item Not Received" dispute. PayPal will review the carrier delivery scans and refund your purchase under PayPal Buyer Protection.
