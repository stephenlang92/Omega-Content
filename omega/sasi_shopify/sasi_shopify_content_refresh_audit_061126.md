### Snippet 4: Cập nhật khối giải đáp thắc mắc thường gặp (FAQs) `[UPDATE]`
> **Q: What was SASI in the Shopify ecosystem?**  
> **A:** SASI stood for **Shopify App Store Index**, a proprietary intelligence tool created by Hey Mantle. It aggregated daily rank tracking across thousands of Shopify apps, calculated category visibility scores, and highlighted "Top Movers" (apps gaining or losing rapid market share) to help developers gauge algorithm updates.
> 
> **Q: Can I still recover my old SASI ranking data from Mantle?**  
> **A:** Unfortunately, no. Mantle's official wind-down terms specified that all stored databases, user dashboards, and historical analytics were permanently scrubbed following the September 30, 2026 final shutdown date.
> 
> **Q: What is the most direct successor to SASI for App Store visibility?**  
> **A:** In its official wind-down documentation, the Mantle team specifically recommended **App Store Pulse** (`appstorepulse.com`) as their designated successor for marketplace indexing, category visibility, and storefront tracking. For developers wanting a 100% free solution covering both keyword ASO and core MRR analytics, **Letsmetrix** is the premier choice.
> 
> **Q: How do I handle app billing now that Mantle Billing is gone?**  
> **A:** All Shopify app developers must now utilize **Shopify App Pricing (native Billing API)**. Shopify's native billing system handles recurring subscriptions, usage-based charges, and multi-currency billing seamlessly without relying on third-party billing middle layers.
